Beyond

Questions we are not sure how to answer yet

Moves from understanding AI to reflecting on how we choose to use it. Through practical questions and interactive experiences, it encourages visitors to examine their own habits, boundaries and level of trust in AI. The aim is to promote a more conscious and responsible relationship with technology.

The Choice

Assistance and dependence are not the same thing. Read each situation below and ask yourself honestly: would you hand this over?

  • Would you let AI choose your next career?
  • Would you let AI write a difficult personal message for you?
  • Would you let AI decide what news you should read?
  • Would you let AI recommend who you should date?
  • Would you let AI help you make a medical decision?
  • Would you let AI be your first source of emotional support?

There is no score and no diagnosis. The point is simply to notice where your own line sits, and whether it moves.
