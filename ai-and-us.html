AI and Us

About this project

About PsychologAI

PsychologAI is an independent project exploring how artificial intelligence may be changing the human mind, our relationships and the way we understand ourselves. It brings together questions about psychology, technology and the future of being human, encouraging people to look beyond what AI can do and consider what it may mean for us.

Why this project exists

Artificial intelligence is becoming part of everyday life, influencing how we learn, communicate, make decisions and understand the world around us. This project aims to explore these changes, ask questions that do not always have simple answers, and encourage people to think more deeply about their relationship with AI.

What this project is not

PsychologAI is an independent project about AI and the human mind, not a clinical service. It does not provide psychological diagnoses, replace professional advice or claim to have all the answers. It is not intended to create fear about AI or promote technology uncritically. Instead, it aims to create space for curiosity, balanced discussion and informed reflection.
